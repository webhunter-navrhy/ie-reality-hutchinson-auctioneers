No. 2 Havenwood, Nursing Home, Ballygunner, Waterford, X91 XED2

Well-Presented 2 Bedroomed semi-detached bungalow in a Popular Over-55s Development

No. 2 Havenwood is a bright and spacious two-bedroom ground floor semi detached property, presented in excellent condition throughout. Situated within the well-established Havenwood retirement development in Ballygunner, this property offers comfortable, low-maintenance living in a peaceful setting, while remaining within easy reach of Waterford City and a host of local amenities.

The accommodation extends to an entrance hall leading to a generous open-plan sitting room and kitchen/dining area, creating a bright and welcoming living space. The property comprises two bedrooms—a spacious double and a single bedroom—both benefiting from half-glazed external timber door opening directly onto the outdoor area, allowing plenty of natural light and easy access outside.

A fully tiled wet room and a convenient hot press complete the accommodation.

Externally, the property enjoys parking and is set within attractive, well-maintained communal grounds.

This is an ideal opportunity for those seeking to down-size while retaining independence in a secure and established community.
